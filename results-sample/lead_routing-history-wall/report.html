Learning curves

What fits in Jev's input

Jev takes at most 32,768 input tokens per call (about 140,000 characters of plain text, fewer for field-heavy text; measured with python -m core.limit, the API answers max_tokens_exceeded above it). Loading every past case into the prompt grows with history: the jev+history arm (every past case, verbatim) was refused by Jev from case 276 on, and never decided again. A top-k lookup stays under it by only ever showing k cases, which is also all it knows at any decision. SenseLab's memory slot does not grow with history (see the main run's report for its size); the history lives outside Jev, where the repair loop can work on it.

The written policy every arm starts from

Enterprise leads (1000+ employees) with a corporate email and buying intent: route to an account executive.
Mid-market leads (100-999 employees) with buying intent (demo, pricing or trial): route to an account executive.
SMB leads (under 100 employees): send to self-serve signup.
Leads from personal email addresses (gmail, yahoo, outlook, etc.): disqualify.
Content downloads and webinar signups with no demo, pricing or trial request: SDR nurture sequence.
